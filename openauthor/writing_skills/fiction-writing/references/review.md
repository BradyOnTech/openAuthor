# Evidence and verification

Judge the actual passage against the author direction, scene intent, supplied story evidence and approved voice. Do not grade the drafter's explanation of what it meant to accomplish. State the book's intended effect rather than imposing a universal literary taste.

Report strengths worth retaining and prioritize the most consequential supported concerns. Quote exact candidate passages. For a fidelity concern, pair them with exact authoritative source passages or supplied facts; a name appearing in both texts is insufficient. Explain the causal or reader effect and suggest the smallest useful repair. Label uncertainty and unavailable context. Finding no issue is valid.

Keep contradictions, unclear staging, missed intent and subjective taste distinct. Avoid a numeric overall quality score and blanket bans on adverbs, exposition, direct dialogue or emotional naming. Check recurring devices in context rather than rejecting an individual sentence on a phrase blacklist.

After a revision, inspect the actual new prose. For each prioritized finding, report fixed, partial, still present or uncertain with fresh evidence, and check for introduced changes to facts, viewpoint, speaker identity or the ending. Stop when meaningful concerns are addressed or the allowed review budget is exhausted. An author can prefer the original, a tie or none; the critic's opinion never applies prose automatically.

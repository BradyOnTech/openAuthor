# Luna dialogue followup assessment

September 29, 2026. This is an assistant assessment of three original-fiction
samples generated using the existing ChatGPT subscription, not an author verdict
or an independent literary-quality certification. The anonymous prose was read
before the direction key; the corpus and earlier findings were already known.

## Result

All three samples stop at the original bread-cutting beat. None adds the closing
dialogue exchange found in all three September 22 variants. All preserve the
spare key in the bellows before dawn, the other key given to Mara's brother,
Ivo's truthful denial and his uncertainty about Mara's knowledge. There is no
confession, discovered key or established private knowledge for Mara.

This is encouraging evidence for explicitly preserving the ending, not a causal
proof of prompt improvement: there was no simultaneous old-prompt control,
sampling is nondeterministic, and only one familiar scene was rerun. The seed
controls review-label shuffling, not model sampling. Author judgments are still
unfilled in [the packet](review.md).

## Candidate review

**A is the safest generated alternative; prefer source/A tie.** It retains the
evasive letter joke, the explicit refusal to drink and the quiet ending. The
opening's extra fragments change cadence without an obvious emotional gain.
“Where it was” is slightly less definite than “where the key was,” although the
referent remains understandable. The rewrite is conservative and readable;
there is little reason to replace the source wholesale.

**B still needs a staging repair.** The cup begins with its chipped rim facing
Ivo, then Mara turns the chip toward him again. This repeats the September 22
problem. “He could not remember” settles an attempted recollection into a failed
one. The final “required no agreement from him” explains the pressure already
carried by her actions. The key facts survive, but this is not the best revision.

**C is more emphatic, not clearly stronger.** “No question. No permission.” makes
the ending louder and more explanatory. “He set it down” drops the explicit
“without drinking,” reducing a meaningful physical distinction. “Couldn't” also
resolves the attempted recollection. The dry dialogue remains, but clipped syntax
alone does not justify these losses of precision and restraint.

`gpt-5.6-luna` calls, no failed comparisons and no malformed self-review warnings.
All three retain protected phrases and lexical markers and fit the preview limit.
Best-effort mode was used, so this is not a strict evidence-validation run.
No API-key inference, private manuscript, model-default change or automatic
application occurred.

The key maps A to Clear, structured epic, B to Quiet and atmospheric, and C to
Kinetic, emotionally intense. These results do not establish a model ranking.
The app now supports targeted feedback on one sample instead of requiring a new
three-way comparison; whether that feature produces better revisions still needs

include fresh scenes and preservation of small physical actions, not repeatedly

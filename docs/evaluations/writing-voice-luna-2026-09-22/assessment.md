# Luna comparison — assistant assessment

2026-09-22. Nine original-fiction variants generated through the existing
ChatGPT subscription connector; no API-key inference or private manuscript.

This assessment was written after reading [review.md](review.md), before
consulting the direction key or generator self-reviews. It is an assistant
reading, not an independent experiment or the author's judgment. The corpus,
prior evaluations and intended directions were already known. Author assessments
remain unreviewed in the generated artifacts.

## The brake — source/A tie

All three preserve six passengers plus the additional boy, Ivo's injured right
hand, his belt suggestion, the interrupted recount, and the failed throw without
a rescue. A explicitly says “before she reached the boy”; B's “one, two” is an
interrupted partial count, **not** a completed recount. The mere presence of
counting progress is not a continuity failure.

A has the clearest generated physical sequence and removes the explicit fear
label. Ivo's dry response remains distinct from Mara's instructions. However,
“Ahead of her” locates a winch the source did not locate, and the extra lurch is
new staging. The revision is usable, not demonstrably better than the source.

B adds more sensation and an explicit command, but places the winch “Somewhere
below” and adds a paragraph explaining the moral meaning of the distance. That
slows the immediate danger and tells us how to interpret a choice already clear.
C places the winch “Above them,” adds a near-detachment of the lever, retains the
fear label, and changes the endpoint from the last *painted* link to “the last
one.” That wording risks conflating a painted threshold with the physical end of
the chain. These are staging uncertainties, not proven contradictions in the
four listed facts; they require author review.

All three add nearly the same river-width comparison. Shared imagery and similar
paragraph breaks suggest limited separation between the directions on this
fixture. Shorter sentences alone do not establish a distinct voice.

## The spare key — source

All three preserve the spare key hidden in the bellows before dawn, the other
key given to Mara's brother, and Ivo's truthful denial of opening the storehouse.
None establishes Mara's private knowledge or reveals the hidden key. The dry
evasion remains recognizable, though B makes the unfortunate-letter joke plainer.

Every variant extends the conversation beyond the original bread-cutting ending.
A adds “You're staying?” / “Apparently.” This appears to settle the visit more
explicitly than the original and introduces avoidable speaker ambiguity. B adds
“I'm not staying,” turning the silent pressure into overt disagreement. C adds
“I am expecting you to eat,” explaining pressure the cup and bread already carry.
These are additional story beats, even though the listed key facts survive.

B and C both introduce the cup with its chip already facing Ivo, then rotate it
to face him again. C even says “again,” without an intervening turn. A and B omit
the source's explicit “without drinking”; the tea touching his teeth is retained,
but the deliberate refusal becomes less definite. A drops the specific fear of
being asked about the brother in favor of “anything else.” C preserves that
specificity and the refusal to drink, making it the closest candidate in those
respects, but its extra ending is not an improvement over the source's restraint.

## The second place — source/A/B near-tie; retain source

All three preserve the eleven-day absence without declaring death, yesterday's
market day, the unsealed letter, the uncracked bowl, the covered second bowl and
the crossed-out line without a replacement. Purposeful washing and checking
survive. A changes essentially one sentence boundary; B changes “put” to “set.”
Neither demonstrates meaningful improvement, and conservative retention is
appropriate for this control.

C changes more rhythm and splits the bell and final sentence into shorter units.
Its “worried it loose” remains grounded in the immediate action, but the added
emphasis does not clearly improve the quiet ending. Prefer the source rather
than treating the number of edits as a quality signal.

## Product decision

Keep Writing Voice as an author-reviewed alternative, not an automatic prose
upgrade. This small run supports useful options and preservation of strong prose;
it does not validate semantic fidelity, broad literary quality or a Luna/Terra
ranking. The next review should prioritize invented ending beats, physical
staging, and whether directions actually sound different. Do not tune prompts
from this single familiar corpus before the author compares the anonymous packet.

# The Moving Map

Mara unrolled the map beside the lamp. A red line crept across the paper, passing streets the city had forgotten. She locked the door and watched it move.

At midnight, the line reached her own door.

# The Clocktower

The clocktower had no hands. Mara climbed its narrow stairs, carrying the map in a brass case. At the top, a bell rang for the missing hour.
